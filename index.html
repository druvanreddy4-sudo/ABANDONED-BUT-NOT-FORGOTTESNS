    About & Preservation

    Digital space archaeology. Hardware left on other worlds is cultural heritage as well as debris. Landing sites hold the earliest evidence of human presence beyond Earth, and future missions should plan their routes and landings so they do not disturb them.

    Data & attribution. Images come live from the NASA Image and Video Library (images.nasa.gov). Base maps come from NASA Solar System Treks (trek.nasa.gov), built on Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter and Viking imagery. Mission facts are summarized from NASA mission records.

    Facts vs. narration. Mission facts are historical. The narration voice and chapter framing are creative storytelling. Map positions are labelled confirmed or approximate.
